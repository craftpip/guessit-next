Parsing guards

Release names are full of traps: numbers that look like episodes but are resolutions, codecs that look like season markers, words that mean two things. These are the rules that keep the parser honest. Each one is locked in by a test — they are features, not accidents.

Big numbers are years or resolutions, never seasons

  • Seasons run 1–60, episodes 1–500. A 4-digit number is a year or a resolution and never becomes a season/episode number.
  • S02 - 2019 → season 2 (the 2019 is a year, not episode 2019). It is never read as a [2, 2019] collection.
  • Season 1 - 720p → season 1 (the 720p is a resolution, not a range end). It is never read as seasons [1, 720].
  • A year in parens — Show - 01 (2021) — is the release year, never “season 2021”.

Codecs are not episodes

  • 1x05 means season 1, episode 5 — but 1920x1080 is a resolution (season must be ≤ 60), and 1 x264 is a codec (episodes 264/265 are excluded from x-notation).
  • Codec decimals are never episodes: Opus 2.0 and DDP2.0 stay codecs, and 2.5 Title… never yields an episode 5.
  • Digit-codes like RIFT_2045 never steal the episode — the real S01E10 in the name wins.

Anime conventions

  • A finale written S2 - 12 END is season 2, episode 12 — never a season range.
  • Part N - <num> next to a season marker names an episode; a bare Part N with no dash-number is a part pack.
  • Final Season - 08 (no “Part”, no episode word) is episode 8 with an unnamed season.
  • S01 1-2 means episodes 1–2 of season 1 (an episode collection), not a season batch.

“Complete” handling

  • A bare complete with no season or movie signals is a complete pack.
  • Season 3 Complete stays a season batch; a year + release-quality movie with “complete” in its title stays a movie.
  • Collection folders (… Season 1-2 S01-S02 …) don't leak into titles: inner files keep the short show title, never the long collection string.

The folder knows best

  • For library files, the folder is authoritative for identity: 10-Conclusion.mkv inside Neutral Show/ takes the folder's title — while 3 Fools (2009).mkv inside its own folder keeps its leading number as part of the movie title.
  • Ranges collapse to the start episode for routing (S01E01-E13 → episode 1), with the end preserved in episodeEnd.
  • Only a real sample marker routes to extra/sample; small file size alone just appends a “sample-size suspect” note to a movie.
  • Extra markers match across underscores (NCED_DVD, file_sample) but never fire inside ordinary words (spider, space).
