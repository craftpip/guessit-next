Parsing guards

Locked in by the test registries — these are features, not accidents. If a regex or S/E rule exists in more than one place, that's a bug: it belongs in src/patterns.js or src/se.js.

Caps and years

  • Seasons are 1–60, episodes 1–500. Four-digit values are years or resolutions and never become season/episode numbers.
  • S02 - 2019 is a season batch (season 2 kept, episode dropped) — never a [2, 2019] collection.
  • Season 1 - 720p is a season batch — never [1, 720].
  • A year in parens (Show - 01 (2021)) is the release year, never Season 2021.

Codecs are not episodes

  • x-notation 1x05 requires season ≤ 60 (kills 1920x1080) and episode ≠ 264/265 (kills 1 x264 codec tails).
  • Codec decimals (Opus 2.0, DDP2.0) are never decimal episodes; 2.5 Title… never yields a bare-episode 5.
  • Underscore-digit codes (RIFT_2045) never steal the episode — the real S01E10 wins.

Anime conventions

  • Finale S2 - 12 END is S02E12 (an episode), never a season range.
  • Part N - <num> with a season anchor is an episode designator; a bare Part N (no dash-number) is a part pack.
  • Final Season - 08 (no “Part”, no episode word) is episode 8 with unnamed season.
  • S01 1-2 means S01E01–S01E02 (episode collection), not a season batch.

“Complete” handling

  • A bare complete with no season/movie signals is a complete pack.
  • Season 3 Complete stays a season batch; a year + release-quality movie with “complete” in its title stays a movie.
  • Complete-collection folders (… Season 1-2 S01-S02 …) are stripped: inner files keep the short show title, never the long collection string.

Library files

  • The folder is authoritative for series identity: 10-Conclusion.mkv inside Neutral Show/ takes the folder's title — while 3 Fools (2009).mkv inside its own folder keeps its leading number as part of the movie title.
  • Ranges collapse to the start episode for routing (S01E01-E13 → episode 1), with the end preserved in episodeEnd.
  • Marker-named samples route to extra/sample; small size alone only appends a “sample-size suspect” note to a movie.
  • Extra markers use underscore-tolerant boundaries (NCED_DVD, file_sample hit) but never fire inside words (spider, space).
